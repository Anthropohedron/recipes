# Ants in Trees

- 2oz soy sauce
- 1.5 tablespoon rice wine
- 1.5 tablespoon sambal chili paste
- 1.5 teaspoon cornstarch
- 1 lb ground pork
---
- 5.3oz mung bean noodles
---
- 6 scallions
---
- 1.5 tablespoon canola oil
- 0.75 C chicken broth

1. Combine soy sauce, rice wine, and chili paste in a medium bowl and whisk until smooth
2. Add cornstarch and whisk again
3. Add pork and mix until fully combined
4. Let stand for 30 minutes, doing the next steps while you wait
---
5. Start water boiling
6. Place noodles in a pot
7. Pour boiling water over noodles until they are submerged (or add noodles to boiling water that has been removed from heat), then let them soak for 20 minutes
8. Cut noodles into 3" to 4" pieces and drain in a colander for 10 minutes
---
9. Chop the scallions, separating into 1/3, all greens, and 2/3, whites and the rest, parts
---
10. Put a 12" pan over high heat for 1 minute then add oil
11. When the oil shimmers add meat mixture and stir constantly for two minutes, breaking it up into very small pieces
12. Add 2/3 scallions (whites) and continue cooking and stirring until browned, about 2 minutes
13. Reduce heat to medium high and add broth
14. Cook until reduced, about 3 minutes
15. Add handfuls of noodles to the pan gradually, mixing with the meat until no liquid remains
16. Top with the 1/3 scallions (greens) and serve immediately
